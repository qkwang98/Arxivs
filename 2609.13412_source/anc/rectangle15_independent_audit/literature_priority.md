# Literature-priority findings and access limits

Checked on 11 September 2026. These findings distinguish correctness from priority.

## Established antecedents

A. M. Vershik and A. Yu. Okounkov, A New Approach to the Representation Theory of the Symmetric Groups. II, arXiv:math/0503040v3 (2005 revision of their 1996 work), explicitly gives products of branching central idempotents in Proposition 1.1 and the diagonal coefficient 1/(a_(i+1)-a_i) in equations (6.3)-(6.5), Propositions 6.1-6.2. The supplied f^gamma/(content difference) trace follows by summing that classical diagonal entry over the f^gamma tableaux on the retained path segment. It should not be advertised as new symmetric-group representation theory.

Primary full text inspected: https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0503040

Pate 1999 explicitly describes tensor-contraction and quadratic-form origins of xi-functions. Huber-Maassen 2021 and Rico-Grinko-Krebs-Zaw 2025 also give operator/Gram formulations of immanant inequalities; the latter's Theorem 4 uses branching/interlacing coefficients for a different separability optimization. These are relevant antecedents, not by themselves proofs that the exact supplied branching inequality already appeared.

## Four requested Pate papers

Pate 1992, Immanant Inequalities and Partition Node Diagrams, JLMS s2-46, 65-80, DOI 10.1112/jlms/s2-46.1.65. The publisher abstract directly states the normalized node-moving inequality used to pass from (5,4,3,3) to (4,4,3,3,1). The full article was not inspected.

Pate 1997, A machine for producing inequalities involving immanants and other generalized matrix functions, LAA 254, 427-466, DOI 10.1016/S0024-3795(96)00512-5. Bibliography verified, including against Wanless's references; full text unavailable in this audit. Equivalence to its general machine remains an unresolved priority check.

Pate 1998, Row Appending Maps, Psi-Functions, and Immanant Inequalities for Hermitian Positive Semi-Definite Matrices, PLMS 76, 307-358, DOI 10.1112/S0024611598000100. Publisher metadata inspected, not the full text. Its (p,q,r,2^s,1^t) family is expressly described in Pate's 1999 abstract.

Pate 1999, Tensor inequalities, xi-functions and inequalities involving immanants, LAA 295, 31-59, DOI 10.1016/S0024-3795(99)00035-X. The primary abstract was inspected. It states the (p,q^w,r,2^s,1^t), 0<=w<=2, class and a separate (n+p,n^k) family for sufficiently large n. The latter's quantitative threshold could not be checked without the full text. Substitution n=3,p=0,k=4 must be checked before conclusively asserting that (3^5) was not already covered; even allowance of p=0 must be checked in the theorem's exact hypotheses.

## Subsequent literature and conclusion

Wanless's 2022 discussion and the November 2025 Rico et al. preprint state an all-partitions bound through order 13. That is useful historical context, but does NOT imply that every individual shape above order 13 was open. No prior explicit proof of the (3^5) case was located in the accessible material.

Relevant primary texts inspected:

The correct priority verdict is provisional: classical ingredients and substantial antecedents are established; absence of an earlier exact branching inequality or of an earlier (3^5) theorem is not established. The new proof's mathematical validity does not depend on resolving these historical questions.
