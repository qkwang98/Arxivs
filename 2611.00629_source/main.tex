\begin{abstract}
We prove Bergeron's Hook-Components and Coefficient-Length conjectures
for the Vandermonde polarization module and full diagonal harmonics in
every rank and number of alphabets. Hook-label characters are elementary
skewings of the alternating character, and the maximal Schur length in
label $\mu$ is $n-\mu_1$. We compute character and dimension polynomials
of diagonal coinvariants in every bosonic--fermionic superspace for
$D\ge3$, $n\ge\max\{D,2D-6\}$, answering Church--Ellenberg--Farb's
character and dimension questions.

For augmented commutative algebras in characteristic zero, we construct
natural two-Schur presentations and filtered free resolutions with one
Schur generator in each homological degree. For polynomial algebras,
specialization to orthogonal idempotents identifies the first unstable
correction for every nonempty tail $\tau$ and degree $D>|\tau|$.
The stable threshold $n\ge D+\tau_1$ is sharp when the row dimension
is at least $\ell(\tau)$. First parabolic algebras recover finite jets,
their multiplication and their maps.

Selected-label lifts give uniform Vandermonde generation through degree
$D$ for $D\ge3$, $n\ge2D-3$, and disjoint-label products generate every
first occurrence for positive integral row weights. Constructive degree
reflection determines all two-column moment homology and generates its
first syzygies from degree $n+1$; two-column Vandermonde generation reduces
to degree $n-1$. Invariants and harmonics separate through the character
range and through degree $n$ in two alphabets. The hook identities also
give the fully graded sign component of the $t=0$, $(1,2)$-superspace
Theta conjecture.
\end{abstract}
