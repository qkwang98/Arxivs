\section{Constructive generation in a linear range}
\label{sec:linear-generation}

The selected-label quotient now becomes an evaluation map for harmonic
polynomials.  Local generated lifts can be multiplied on disjoint label
sets, and a two-label kernel saves one auxiliary label.  These two
operations prove the long-row and odd-row criteria.  The hook identities
then reduce the uniform range to one explicit rank-five lift.

\begin{lemma}[Selected evaluation]\label{lem:selected-evaluation}
Let $V=K^k$, $B=\operatorname{Sym}V$, $\mathfrak m=B_+$, $\mu=(t,\tau)$,
$|\tau|=s$, and $n=t+s$.
If $D\ge s$ and $n\ge D+\tau_1$, setting the auxiliary variables to zero
identifies $\Harm_{n,k}[D]_\mu^{S_t}$ with
$(\mathfrak m^{\otimes s})_{D,\tau}$.
More generally, in any range, suppose the span of selected
restrictions of $\Pol_{n,k}[D]^{S_t}$ contains the whole
row-$\lambda$, selected-label-$\tau$ component of
$(\mathfrak m^{\otimes s})_D$.  Then the entire row-$\lambda$,
label-$\mu$ harmonic component is generated.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Let $A_s=B^{\otimes s}=\mathfrak m^{\otimes s}\oplus W$, where $W$
contains tensors with a unit position. The minimum-occupancy argument
of Lemma~\ref{lem:minimum-occupancy-cover} gives the selected surjection
$\pi:A_s\to R_{n,k}^{S_t}$, eliminates $W$ on label $\mu$, and
identifies $(S^\mu)^{S_t}$ with $S^\tau$.
For a selected polynomial $f$ and an $S_t$-invariant harmonic $g$,
\[
 \langle\pi(f),g\rangle
   =\langle f,g(X_1,\ldots,X_s,0,\ldots,0)\rangle.
\]
Restriction is therefore the Fischer adjoint of this cover and is
injective. The occupancy summands are orthogonal, so on label $\mu$
it takes values in the fully occupied summand. In the stable range
Theorem~\ref{thm:stable-augmented-multiplicities} makes it an isomorphism.

For the general assertion, suppose a coinvariant class in the
indicated global component annihilates all generated harmonics.
Take a nonzero $S_t$-fixed vector in this annihilator and choose
a selected representative $f$, projected to row type $\lambda$
and selected-label type $\tau$.  Pairing against the prescribed
restrictions shows that the fully occupied part of $f$ is zero.
Hence $f\in W$.  But $\pi(W)$ has no global label $\mu$, a
contradiction.  Nondegeneracy of the coinvariant--harmonic pairing
proves generation.
\end{proof}

We will build prescribed restrictions by multiplying harmonics on
disjoint sets of labels.  Each local lift uses auxiliary labels whose variables are later set to zero.

\begin{theorem}[Long first rows]
\label{thm:long-first-row}
For every degree $D\ge1$ and every label $\mu\vdash n$,
\[
 \mu_1\ge D-1
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \Pol_{n,k}[D]_\mu=\Harm_{n,k}[D]_\mu
\]
in every number $k$ of alphabets.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For $D\le2$, every row partition has length at most two.
Lemma~\ref{lem:stability} reduces its highest-weight vectors to two
alphabets, where Haiman's operator theorem applies. The same reduction
will generate the two-row local lifts below. Assume $D\ge3$ and put
$t=D-1$. If $n<t$
there is no label in the stated range; if $n=t$, only the trivial
label qualifies, and its positive-degree harmonic part is zero.
We may therefore take $s=n-t\ge1$ selected labels.

The first selected relations have degree $t+1=D$.  In that degree
all their inputs are linear, so their row type is $(D)$.
The selected quotient, and hence its dual harmonic restriction,
is an isomorphism on every other row type.  We will generate all
of these row components with exactly $D-1$ auxiliary labels.

Fix an occupied selected multidegree
$d_1+\cdots+d_r=D$, with each $d_i>0$, and write
\[
 T=\bigotimes_{i=1}^r\Sym^{d_i}V,\qquad
 K_{ij}=\ker\bigl(\Sym^{d_i}V\otimes\Sym^{d_j}V
                         \longrightarrow\Sym^{d_i+d_j}V\bigr).
\]
The multiplication map gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:pair-kernels-symmetric-quotient}
 T\Big/\sum_{i<j}
       K_{ij}\otimes\bigotimes_{h\ne i,j}\Sym^{d_h}V
                    \ \cong\ \Sym^D V.
\end{equation}
Indeed, realize $T$ as the quotient of $V^{\otimes D}$ by
permutations within its blocks of sizes $d_i$.  Quotienting by
$K_{ij}$ imposes symmetry on the union of the $i$th and $j$th
blocks.  These block-pair permutations generate all of $S_D$.
Thus the pair kernels span every row component of $T$ other than
$(D)$.

Put $d=d_i+d_j$.  By Pieri, $K_{ij}$ has at most two rows and
contains no copy of $(d)$.  At local rank $d+1$, take two selected
labels and $d-1$ auxiliary labels.  Its first selected relation has degree
$d$ and row type $(d)$, so restriction has a harmonic lift of
every vector in $K_{ij}$.  Haiman's theorem generates these lifts.
Each remaining selected factor $\Sym^{d_h}V$ has a generated lift
with $d_h$ auxiliary labels: in one row, differentiate the Vandermonde on
$x,y_0,\ldots,y_{d_h-1}$ by
$\prod_{q=0}^{d_h-1}\partial_{y_q}^{q}$ and set the auxiliary labels to
zero.  The result is a nonzero multiple of $x^{d_h}$; row
polarization gives the whole symmetric power.

The total number of auxiliary labels is
\[
                  (d-1)+\sum_{h\ne i,j}d_h=D-1.
\]
Multiply these lifts on disjoint label sets using
Theorem~\ref{thm:r5-disjoint-products}, and average over the
auxiliary labels; unused selected labels contribute constants.
Restriction sets every auxiliary label to zero, so averaging
preserves the prescribed selected polynomial.  Equation
\eqref{eq:pair-kernels-symmetric-quotient}, summed over selected
multidegrees, now generates every non-one-row harmonic component
fixed by $S_t$.  The ordinary operator theorem and row polarization
generate the one-row component.

Injectivity of harmonic restriction gives
$\Pol_{n,k}[D]^{S_t}=\Harm_{n,k}[D]^{S_t}$.
Every $S^\mu$ with $\mu_1\ge t$ has a nonzero $S_t$-fixed vector
by branching.  Exactness of invariants therefore gives the stated
equality on every such label.
\end{proof}

Alternating pairs save one auxiliary label each.  Placing such pairs in the
columns of a label tail makes the savings compatible with its
Young symmetrizer.

\begin{theorem}[Odd-row generation criterion]
\label{thm:odd-row-generation}
For every $\mu\vdash n$,
\[
 D\le\mu_1+\mu_3+\mu_5+\cdots
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \Pol_{n,k}[D]_\mu=\Harm_{n,k}[D]_\mu .
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Write $\mu=(t,\tau)$ and pair adjacent boxes vertically in every
column of $\tau$. The number of pairs is
$p=\tau_2+\tau_4+\cdots$.
An alternating pair of positive-degree polynomials of total degree $d$
has a generated lift on $d+1$ labels, with two selected labels.
Indeed, its selected-sign component has no degree-$d$ relation:
the first selected relations have degree $d$ and are $S_2$-invariant.
Its row types have length at most two, so Haiman's operator theorem
generates the harmonic lift. Such a pair costs $d-1$ auxiliary labels;
a singleton of degree $d$ costs $d$.

The disjoint products therefore use $D-p$ auxiliary labels. Applying the
Young symmetrizer for $\tau$ spans its whole fully occupied selected
component: each prealternated pair multiplies the column
antisymmetrization by two. Averaging over the auxiliary labels and Lemma~\ref{lem:selected-evaluation} prove generation whenever $t\ge D-p$, as asserted.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Four universal tail degrees]
\label{lem:four-tail-degrees}
The alternating component is generated in every degree $d\le n+2$.
Every hook label of tail $s$ is generated in degrees $d\le s+3$.
More generally, if $\mu=\tau[n]$, $s=|\tau|$, then
\[
 0\le D-s\le3,\qquad n\ge D+\tau_1
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \Pol_{n,k}[D]_\mu=\Harm_{n,k}[D]_\mu .
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The ambient sign multiplicity is
$\bigwedge^n(\operatorname{Sym}V)$.
Through degree $n+2$, its nonlinear factors, after removing exterior
powers of $V$ and the possible constant polynomial, are among
\[
 \operatorname{Sym}^2V,\quad \operatorname{Sym}^3V,\quad
 \bigwedge^2\operatorname{Sym}^2V,\quad
 \operatorname{Sym}^4V,\quad
 \operatorname{Sym}^3V\otimes\operatorname{Sym}^2V,\quad
 \bigwedge^3\operatorname{Sym}^2V .
\]
The last two decompose as
$\Schur_5V\oplus \Schur_{41}V\oplus \Schur_{32}V$ and
$\Schur_{411}V\oplus \Schur_{33}V$, respectively;
$\bigwedge^2\operatorname{Sym}^2V=\Schur_{31}V$.
Vertical Pieri shows that none of these, after tensoring with an
exterior power of $V$, has both $\lambda_2\ge3$ and $\lambda_3\ge2$.
Corollary~\ref{cor:r2-defect-support} therefore rules out a sign
defect. The exact hook-defect identities give the hook assertion.

Now consider a column of $\tau$ of height $c$ and degree $d$.
Since the total excess is at most three, $c\le d\le c+3$.
The stable evaluation statement at local rank $d+1$ identifies
its fully occupied alternating component with the hook
$(d+1-c,1^c)$, which is generated by the assertion just proved.
It costs $d+1-c$ auxiliary labels. Summing over columns costs
$D-s+\tau_1$. Disjoint products, Young symmetrization and Lemma~\ref{lem:selected-evaluation} prove the final claim.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Uniform linear generation]
\label{thm:linear-generation}
For $D\ge3$ and $n\ge2D-3$, in every number of alphabets,
\[
 \Pol_{n,k}[d]=\Harm_{n,k}[d]\qquad(0\le d\le D).
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
First prove the assertion in degree $D$.
Write $\mu=(t,\tau)$, $s=|\tau|$. Occupancy gives $s\le D$.
If $t\ge D-1$, apply Theorem~\ref{thm:long-first-row}.
Otherwise $t\le D-2$, and $n\ge2D-3$ forces $s\ge D-1$.
For $s=D$, Lemma~\ref{lem:four-tail-degrees} applies because
$n=D+t\ge D+\tau_1$.  For $s=D-1$, necessarily
$t=D-2$ and $n=2D-3$.  The same lemma applies if
$\tau_1\le D-3$.  The sole remaining label is therefore
\[
                         \mu=(D-2,D-2,1).
\]

Put $s=D-1$.  We reach its one exceptional row by combining a
three-selected-label harmonic at rank five with linear lifts.
The general part of Lemma~\ref{lem:selected-evaluation} lets us
work in the fully occupied selected space: spanning that space
by restrictions suffices at this rank as well.
The degree-$s+1$ part of $\Schur_{(s-1,1)}(\mathfrak m)$ has one
quadratic slot. Its only row type of length greater than two which
is not a row hook is $(s-2,2,1)$, with multiplicity one; it occurs
only for $s\ge4$. This follows by removing one corner of $(s-1,1)$
and applying the horizontal Pieri rule with $S_2V$.
For $s<4$, Haiman's theorem and row-hook generation therefore
finish the proof.  Assume $s\ge4$ and $\dim V\ge3$, so the
exceptional row is present.

At rank five, a local selected-three vector of selected type
$(2,1)$ and row type $(2,1,1)$ has a harmonic lift: the degree-four
relations have at most two rows.  Indeed, they come either from
a degree-three relation multiplied by a linear form, with row
source $\Sym^3V\otimes V$, or from a relation on two linear
inputs and one quadratic input, with row source
$\Sym^2V\otimes\Sym^2V$.  Neither source contains $(2,1,1)$.
Proposition~\ref{prop:row-hook-generation} generates this lift.
Multiply it with $s-3$ linear lifts. The number of auxiliary labels is
$2+(s-3)=s-1=D-2$.

The local row projection and selected Young projection must both
survive. In the realization
$\Schur_{(s-1,1)}\mathfrak m
=\ker(\operatorname{Sym}^{s-1}\mathfrak m\otimes\mathfrak m
\to\operatorname{Sym}^s\mathfrak m)$, the selected projection is
\[
 h\otimes\bigl(w\otimes(a\wedge b)\bigr)
             \longmapsto (hwa)\otimes b-(hwb)\otimes a.
\]
It symmetrizes the first row and preserves the distinguished last box.
The quadratic factor $w\in\Sym^2V$ varies independently, so
one can check its $x^2$ component separately.
Set
$W=\operatorname{Sym}^2V$ and choose
independent $x,y,z\in V$. In $W\otimes\bigwedge^2V$ put
\[
 v=x^2\otimes(y\wedge z)-xy\otimes(x\wedge z)
                         +xz\otimes(x\wedge y).
\]
This is a highest vector of type $(2,1,1)$, and
\[
 E_{21}v=xy\otimes(y\wedge z)-y^2\otimes(x\wedge z)
                         +yz\otimes(x\wedge y).
\]
Thus
\[
 x^{s-4}y\otimes v-x^{s-3}\otimes E_{21}v
\]
is highest of type $(s-2,2,1)$.
The image of the displayed highest vector has nonzero $w=x^2$
component
\[
 x^2\otimes
 (x^{s-4}y^2\otimes z-x^{s-4}yz\otimes y).
\]
The second summand has no such quadratic factor, so it cannot cancel.
The target is $\operatorname{Sym}^2V\otimes\operatorname{Sym}^{s-2}V\otimes V$;
its first factor records the quadratic factor.
Thus the selected Young projection is nonzero on the generated
products.  Row polarization reaches the whole multiplicity-one
component, and averaging over auxiliary labels preserves its selected restriction.
Lemma~\ref{lem:selected-evaluation} gives generation of this last
row-and-label component.
All remaining rows are generated by Haiman or row-hook generation.

This proves degree $D$.  For $3\le d\le D$ the same hypothesis
gives $n\ge2d-3$; degrees zero, one and two follow from the
ordinary and bivariate operator theorems.
\end{proof}

The labelwise criteria also locate every possible remaining defect.
If $(\Harm_{n,k}[D]/\Pol_{n,k}[D])_\mu\ne0$, then
\[
 n-D+1\le\mu_1\le D-2,
 \qquad \mu_1+\mu_3+\mu_5+\cdots\le D-1.
\]
Indeed, occupancy gives $n-\mu_1\le D$. Equality would make degree
$D$ a first occurrence, which is generated by
Theorem~\ref{thm:r5-complete-first}; the other bounds are
Theorems~\ref{thm:long-first-row} and~\ref{thm:odd-row-generation}.
