\section{Introduction}

Bergeron's Hook-Components and Coefficient-Length conjectures
\cite[Conjectures~2 and~3]{Bergeron} describe how the symmetric-group
isotypes of multivariate diagonal harmonics fit together.  The first
recovers every hook-label character from the alternating character by
elementary skewing.  The second gives the exact number of alphabets
needed to retain every Schur coefficient in an arbitrary label
isotype.  We prove both conjectures for the Vandermonde polarization
module in all ranks and for full diagonal harmonics, with derivative
maps and finite-alphabet reconstruction.

We also answer Church--Ellenberg--Farb's eventual-character and
dimension-polynomial questions \cite[Problems~1.10 and~1.12]{ChurchEllenbergFarb2015},
construct natural Schur presentations and resolutions for tensor
coinvariants of arbitrary augmented algebras, and prove uniform
Vandermonde generation ranges. An idempotent specialization identifies
every first unstable character correction.
The hook transfer gives the fully graded Theta sign component.

\subsection{The hook-component and coefficient-length conjectures}

Let $K$ be a field of characteristic zero, let $n,k\ge1$, and put $V=K^k$.
Define
\[
 P_{n,k}=\Sym(V\otimes K^n)
       =K[x_i^{(a)}:i\in[n],\ a\in[k]],\qquad
 R_{n,k}=P_{n,k}/\langle(P_{n,k}^{S_n})_+\rangle.
\]
The row action of $\GL(V)$ commutes with the label action of $S_n$.
We use $\lambda$ for row partitions and $\mu$ for label partitions,
with Schur modules $\mathbb S_\lambda V$ and Specht modules $S^\mu$:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:character-decomposition}
 R_{n,k}\cong
 \bigoplus_{\mu\vdash n}\ \bigoplus_{\ell(\lambda)\le k}
 c_{\lambda,\mu}^{(n)}\,
                 \mathbb S_\lambda V\otimes S^\mu.
\end{equation}
Let $\Harm_{n,k}$ be the apolar harmonic model of $R_{n,k}$, and
let $\Pol_{n,k}$ be generated from the Vandermonde determinant
$\Delta_n(x^{(1)})$ by all partial
derivatives and generalized polarizations
\[
 E_{a,b}^{(r)}=\sum_{i=1}^n
             x_i^{(a)}\partial_{x_i^{(b)}}^r,\qquad r\ge1.
\]
This is Bergeron's module $\mathcal E_n^{\langle k\rangle}$
\cite[Section~2]{Bergeron}, in the general polarization framework of
\cite[Section~3.1]{r5-Blandin}.  Haiman's operator theorem gives
$\Pol_{n,2}=\Harm_{n,2}$~\cite[Theorem~4.2]{Haiman}.
For $\mathcal M=\Harm$ or $\Pol$, let $C_\mu^{\mathcal M}$ be the
stable row character of its label-$\mu$ multiplicity and put
$A_n^{\mathcal M}=C_{(1^n)}^{\mathcal M}$.  Here stability refers to
adjoining alphabets, with $n$ fixed, and $e_j^\perp$ is adjoint to
multiplication by $e_j$ for the Hall inner product.

\begin{maintheorem}[Bergeron's Hook-Components and Coefficient-Length conjectures]
\label{thm:intro-hooks}
For every $n\ge1$ and $\mathcal M=\Harm,\Pol$,
\begin{align*}
 C_{(j+1,1^{n-j-1})}^{\mathcal M}
          &=e_j^\perp A_n^{\mathcal M} &&(0\le j<n),\\
 \max\{\ell(\lambda):[s_\lambda]C_\mu^{\mathcal M}\ne0\}
          &=n-\mu_1 &&(\mu\vdash n).
\end{align*}
In $k\ge1$ alphabets the largest length is
$\min\{k,n-\mu_1\}$.  The empty row partition has length zero.
\end{maintheorem}

The length bound is attained by generated row-hook multiplicity
spaces (Theorem~\ref{thm:length}). The hook identities are realized
by exteriorization and derivative maps
(Theorems~\ref{thm:full-hook} and~\ref{thm:r2-operator-hook-transfer}).

The same calculation proves the nontrivial hook-label cases of
Bergeron's Conjecture~4, with the hook--hook formula
\eqref{eq:hook-hook-formula}. It uses the Fields character theorem
\cite{MRW} and the superharmonic operator theorem \cite{RW}.

The identities also make Bergeron's finite-alphabet reconstruction
unconditional: for $n\ge3$, the hook-label characters in
$\lfloor(n-1)/2\rfloor$ alphabets determine $A_n^{\mathcal M}$
(Theorem~\ref{thm:reconstruction}). The support bound
$\lambda'_1+\lambda'_2\le n-1$ gives alternating and hook-label
generation for $n\le6$ (Corollary~\ref{cor:r2-defect-support}).

For one bosonic and two fermionic alphabets, the transfer proves
the fully graded sign component of the $t=0$ Theta conjecture
\cite[Conjecture~8.2]{DIV2021}
(Theorem~\ref{thm:one-boson-two-fermion-sign}). At $q=1$ its character
is $(1+u+v)^{n-1}$ and its dimension is $3^{n-1}$.
The known prediction \cite[Theorem~5.6]{INV2024},
recorded in \cite[Theorem~8.3]{Lentfer12}, is realized by the hook
identity and diagonal supersymmetry \cite{Lentfer}.

For a nontrivial label $\mu=(c,\tau)$, the maximal-length row-hook
multiplicities form $\mathbb S_{\tau'}K^c$, with principal grading
shifted by one. Explicit harmonic operators realize its auxiliary
$\GL_c$ action and Lefschetz maps
(Theorem~\ref{thm:maximal-hook-action}).

\subsection{The character questions of Church--Ellenberg--Farb}

Problem~1.10 of \cite{ChurchEllenbergFarb2015} asks for the
multigraded symmetric-group characters in every number of alphabets,
at least for sufficiently large $n$.  Problem~1.12 asks for explicit
dimension polynomials whose existence follows from FI-module theory.
Write $X_j(\sigma)$ for the number of $j$-cycles of $\sigma\in S_n$.

\begin{maintheorem}[Full characters and dimension polynomials]
\label{thm:intro-full-characters}
Let $\mathbf b=(b_1,\ldots,b_k)\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}^k$ have total degree $D\ge3$.
If $n\ge\max\{D,2D-6\}$, then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:intro-full-character}
 \operatorname{tr}\!\left(\sigma\mid(R_{n,k})_{\mathbf b}\right)
 = [\mathbf q^{\mathbf b}]
   \prod_{\substack{\alpha\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}^k\\0<|\alpha|\le D}}
        (1-\mathbf q^\alpha)
   \prod_{j=1}^{D}\prod_{a=1}^{k}
                         (1-q_a^j)^{-X_j(\sigma)}.
\end{equation}
The moment Koszul complex is exact in positive homological degrees
in this range.  The formula also holds for $D\le1$, $n\ge1$,
and for $D=2$, $n\ge2$.
\end{maintheorem}

At the identity this gives the dimension polynomial in every
multidegree, with a range uniform in the number of alphabets.
Church--Ellenberg--Farb's theory establishes
eventual polynomiality and representation stability; subsequent
quantitative bounds include \cite{Bahran2024,Borisov2026}.
This establishes Bergeron's proposed low-degree Euler product
\cite[Section~4, equation~(33)]{Bergeron2013} in the displayed range.

The quadratic model also computes the first-occurrence degree and
the next two degrees of every label in every rank, and the entire two-column row sector
(Corollary~\ref{cor:first-three-character-layers}). These degrees
may lie beyond the uniform stable range.

Orthogonal Littlewood theory \cite{SSW2013} and the Schur relation
confine positive homology to boundary rows. Exactness in the first
possible degree removes an entire boundary before any case calculation. Wang's dimension polynomial
\cite[Theorem~5.1]{Wang2026Polynomiality} and a free cover give bivariate
exactness; the Fields character treats row hooks. At $n=2D-6$,
one explicit nonzero cubic image finishes the proof, independently
of operator generation. Supersymmetry transfers the formula to every
bosonic--fermionic superspace \cite{Lentfer}.

In every rank, two-column moment homology is acyclic at degree $n$
and reflects degrees $D>n$ to $2n-D$ by an explicit cycle map
(Theorem~\ref{thm:two-column-reflection}). Under this reflection,
multiplication becomes symplectic contraction. Thus all two-column
first syzygies are generated from their degree-$(n+1)$ layer
(Corollary~\ref{cor:two-column-syzygy-generation}).
Invariants and harmonics separate through the character range,
and through degree $n$ on row hooks and partitions of length at most two
(Corollary~\ref{cor:uniform-separation}).

\subsection{Natural stable multiplicity spaces}

The underlying presentation applies to every augmented commutative
algebra $B=K\oplus\mathfrak m$ in characteristic zero. The following
stable consequence requires only a connected positive grading.  Define
\[
 Q_n(B)=B^{\otimes n}/
       \bigl\langle(B^{\otimes n})^{S_n}_+\bigr\rangle.
\]
For a partition $\tau\vdash s$, write $\tau[n]=(n-s,\tau)$
when this is a partition, and set $\tau_1=0$ for the empty partition.

\Needspace{9\baselineskip}
\begin{samepage}
\begin{maintheorem}[Natural stable multiplicities]
\label{thm:intro-stable-spaces}
For $D\ge s$ and $n\ge D+\tau_1$, there is an isomorphism
\[
 \Hom_{S_n}\!\left(S^{\tau[n]},Q_n(B)_D\right)
                   \cong(\mathbb S_\tau\mathfrak m)_D.
\]
After fixing the one-dimensional Pieri branching line, the
isomorphism is natural for graded augmented algebra maps and
auxiliary symmetries preserving the grading and augmentation.
\end{maintheorem}
\end{samepage}

Multiplication enters these multiplicity spaces through the canonical
relation map $\beta_{\mu,B}$, whose source for $\mu=(t,\tau)$ is
$\mathbb S_{(t+1,\tau_2,\ldots)}\mathfrak m$. In a connected positive
grading, its first possible contribution has degree $n+1-\tau_1$.
The map commutes with arbitrary augmented algebra maps and vanishes
when $\mathfrak m^2=0$ (Theorem~\ref{thm:two-schur-presentation}).
For $B=\Sym V$, the multiplicity character is
$[s_\tau[\sum_{j\ge1}h_j(V)]]_D$.

For nonempty $\tau$, the first correction is exact in \emph{every} degree $D>s$.
At $n=D+\tau_1-1$, the kernel of the canonical Schur cover is
\[
                \mathbb S_{(D-s+\tau_1,\tau_2,\ldots)}V.
\]
It is nonzero whenever $\dim V\ge\ell(\tau)$, proving sharpness of
the stable threshold in every such degree
(Theorem~\ref{thm:first-unstable-specht-wall}). The proof specializes
to orthogonal idempotents: their tensor moment complex contracts on
every nonzero color. Naturality then detects the polynomial relation.

The relation extends to a natural filtered resolution with one
Schur generator in each homological degree
(Theorem~\ref{thm:full-schur-resolution}, proved in
Appendix~\ref{app:schur-resolution}). The idempotent argument gives
injectivity at every first possible polynomial degree except the
invariant first relation (Theorem~\ref{thm:polynomial-schur-fronts}).

The first parabolic algebra retains multiplication:
\[
                  Q_n(B)^{S_{n-1}}\cong B/\mathfrak m^n.
\]
Equivariant maps recover the augmented jet maps
(Theorem~\ref{thm:finite-jet-full-faithfulness}), and compatible towers
recover the formal completion. Thus these algebras retain the
multiplication that stable multiplicities forget.

\subsection{Constructive Vandermonde generation}

Selected-label evaluation and disjoint products construct harmonic
lifts. Write $\mathcal M_{n,k}[D]_\mu$ for the label-$\mu$ isotype
in total degree $D$.

\begin{maintheorem}[Uniform multivariate Vandermonde generation]
\label{thm:intro-linear}
In every number of alphabets, either condition
\[
 \mu_1\ge D-1
 \qquad\text{or}\qquad
 D\le\mu_1+\mu_3+\mu_5+\cdots
\]
implies $\Pol_{n,k}[D]_\mu=\Harm_{n,k}[D]_\mu$.
For $D\ge3$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:intro-linear-range}
 n\ge2D-3
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \Pol_{n,k}[d]=\Harm_{n,k}[d]\qquad(0\le d\le D).
\end{equation}
For every $n,k$, every label, and every strictly positive integral
row-weight vector, the full label isotype at its first weighted
occurrence is generated. The first occurrence
means the least weighted degree in which the label appears.
\end{maintheorem}

Thus every degree through $\lfloor(n+3)/2\rfloor$ is generated,
uniformly in the number of alphabets.
A colored determinant map turns the curvilinear Quot degeneration
of B\'erczi--Svendsen \cite{r5-BS} into disjoint-label multiplication
and every positive-weight first occurrence.
Pair kernels save auxiliary labels, column alternation gives the
odd-row criterion, and the hook identities leave one family,
$(D-2,D-2,1)$, settled by an explicit rank-five lift.

Two-column Vandermonde generation reduces to the single degree
$n-1$. For $n\ge6$, it suffices to test the remaining rows in
$n-3$ alphabets; the cases $n\le5$ are already generated
(Proposition~\ref{prop:two-column-middle-test}).
