\section{Selected-label elimination}\label{sec:selected-quotients}

Let $K$ be a field, let $n!$ be invertible in $K$, and let
$\varepsilon:B\to K$ be an augmented commutative $K$-algebra, with
$\mathfrak m=\ker\varepsilon$.  Put
\[
 Q_n(B)=B^{\otimes n}/
  \big\langle(B^{\otimes n})^{S_n}\cap
                  \ker(\varepsilon^{\otimes n})\big\rangle.
\]
For $b\in B$, write $b^{(i)}$ for its image in the $i$th tensor
factor.  The notation $S_{n-r}$ below means the subgroup fixing the
first $r$ tensor factors.  We call these $r$ factors selected and
the remaining factors auxiliary.  When $B$ is the coordinate algebra
of a pointed affine scheme, $Q_n(B)$ is its diagonal coinvariant
algebra at the basepoint.

Traces on the auxiliary factors can be eliminated using the global invariant traces.
The relations left after elimination are complete symmetric
functions on the selected factors.  The proof starts from the
fundamental trace presentation \cite[Theorem~1]{Domokos2017}; its
orbit-sum form makes both the resulting relations and the
characteristic hypothesis transparent.

\begin{theorem}[Exact selected-label presentation]
\label{thm:universal-selected-trace-presentation}
For $1\le r\le n$, set $m=n-r+1$.  Inclusion of the first $r$
tensor factors induces a natural algebra isomorphism
\begin{equation}\label{eq:universal-selected-trace-presentation}
 Q_n(B)^{S_{n-r}}
 \cong
 \frac{B^{\otimes r}}
 {\big\langle
     h_m\bigl(b^{(1)},\ldots,b^{(r)}\bigr):b\in\mathfrak m
       \big\rangle}.
\end{equation}
Equivalently, one may use every multilinear polarization of the
displayed relation, allowing arbitrary inputs from $\mathfrak m$.
The isomorphism is equivariant for $S_r$ and for every group acting
on $B$ by augmentation-preserving algebra automorphisms.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Let $0\le k\le n$ and put $\operatorname{TS}^k(B)
=(B^{\otimes k})^{S_k}$.  In the polynomial algebra
$\operatorname{Sym}_K(\mathfrak m)$ write $T_b$ for the generator
depending $K$-linearly on $b\in\mathfrak m$.  For $j\ge1$ define
\[
 \Psi_j(b_1,\ldots,b_j)=
 \sum_{\pi\in\Pi_j}(-1)^{j-|\pi|}
       \prod_{C\in\pi}(|C|-1)!
       \prod_{C\in\pi}T_{\prod_{i\in C}b_i},
\]
where $\Pi_j$ is the set of set partitions of $\{1,\ldots,j\}$.
Under $T_b\mapsto\sum_{i=1}^k b^{(i)}$, inclusion--exclusion on
the partition lattice identifies $\Psi_j$ with
\[
 \sum_{\substack{i_1,\ldots,i_j\in\{1,\ldots,k\}\\
                  \text{pairwise distinct}}}
      b_1^{(i_1)}\cdots b_j^{(i_j)}.
\]
In particular $\Psi_{k+1}$ maps to zero.  Multiplication by one
further trace gives the recursion
\begin{equation}\label{eq:trace-recursion-universal}
 \Psi_{j+1}(b_1,\ldots,b_j,c)
 =T_c\Psi_j(b_1,\ldots,b_j)
  -\sum_{a=1}^j
      \Psi_j(b_1,\ldots,b_{a-1},b_ac,b_{a+1},\ldots,b_j)
\end{equation}
which shows that all $\Psi_j$ with $j>k$ follow from
$\Psi_{k+1}$ and that the quotient by these relations is spanned
by $\Psi_j$, $0\le j\le k$ (with $\Psi_0=1$).

Choose a $K$-basis of $\mathfrak m$ and append $1$ to obtain a
basis of $B$.  The images of these $\Psi_j$, indexed by multisets
of $j$ basis elements, are nonzero scalar multiples of the orbit
basis of $\operatorname{TS}^k(B)$.  Their scalar factors are
products of factorials at most $k!$, and hence are invertible.
They are therefore linearly independent.  This proves
\[
 \operatorname{TS}^k(B)
 \cong\operatorname{Sym}_K(\mathfrak m)/
       \langle\Psi_{k+1}(b_1,\ldots,b_{k+1})\rangle.
\]
In particular, symmetric tensors are generated by the traces of
elements of $\mathfrak m$.

Now take $k=n-r$.  Before imposing the full $S_n$-invariants, the
$S_k$-invariant algebra is
$B^{\otimes r}\otimes\operatorname{TS}^k(B)$.
Reynolds averaging shows that the intersection of the full
invariant-generated ideal with this algebra is generated there by
the same invariant traces.  Thus the quotient imposes
\[
 T_b=-p_r(b),\qquad
 p_r(b)=\sum_{i=1}^r b^{(i)}\qquad(b\in\mathfrak m).
\]
Under this substitution every set-partition term of $\Psi_m$ has
sign $(-1)^m$.  Removing that common sign leaves
\[
 \sum_{\pi\in\Pi_m}
       \prod_{C\in\pi}(|C|-1)!
       \prod_{C\in\pi}p_r\Bigl(\prod_{i\in C}b_i\Bigr).
\]
The complete-symmetric generating identity identifies this with
the coefficient of $t_1\cdots t_m$ in
\[
 h_m\left(\sum_{a=1}^m t_ab_a^{(1)},\ldots,
           \sum_{a=1}^m t_ab_a^{(r)}\right).
\]
Polarization is valid because $m!$ is invertible.  Elimination of
all the $T_b$ therefore gives exactly
\eqref{eq:universal-selected-trace-presentation}.  Every construction
commutes with augmented algebra maps and with the stated actions.
\end{proof}
