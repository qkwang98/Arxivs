We construct higher additive noncommutative motives of stable
$(\infty,n)$-categories, extending the additive motives of
Blumberg, Gepner, and Tabuada and the secondary theory of
Mazel-Gee and Stern.
We formulate
a notion of exact sequences and establish the universal
property of the resulting additive motives.
We give two equivalent constructions: a localization of
enriched presheaves and a weighted Waldhausen
$S$-construction on complete exact flags.
We then compute their Hom objects, obtaining ordinary
K-theory at the first level and lower additive motives
recursively at higher levels.
Our results recover the classical and secondary theories,
provide coherent multiplicative structures, and yield
an endofunctor on the category of Stefanich rings.
In short, higher motives are higher K-theory.
