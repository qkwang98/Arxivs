\title[Koszul duality and determinant formulas]{Koszul duality and
determinant formulas for affine hyperplane arrangements}
\author{Jens Niklas Eberhardt}
\author{Carl Mautner}
\date{9 November 2026}

\begin{abstract}
For real affine hyperplane arrangements, we prove Lam's determinant
conjecture for the inverse Varchenko matrix restricted to bounded chambers.  The proof combines Lam's comparison
of twisted intersection forms with the period determinant formula of
Varchenko, Loeser--Sabbah and Douai--Terao.
For normal crossing arrangements, we introduce the taut path algebra and the chamber intersection algebra and establish a Koszul duality between
them, yielding an
explicit multivariate formula for the inverse Varchenko matrix.
After specializing the parameters to $q$, its restriction to bounded chambers
is a scalar multiple of the $q$-intersection matrix, proving a determinant
conjecture of the authors.  We also discuss how the Koszul duality
results generalize to affine oriented matroids.
\end{abstract}
