%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%% ABSTRACT %%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{abstract}
General linear groups are well-studied Lie groups that encode the linear symmetries of a finitely-dimensional linear space. In differential geometry, they are an important ingredient of various constructions, including fibre bundles and the frame bundle of a principal bundle. They also appear in algebraic geometry as affine group schemes. Their Lie algebra, known as the general linear Lie algebra, is isomorphic to the Lie algebra of square matrices, whose Lie bracket is given by the commutator. Building on previous work on Lie groups, in this paper we introduce general linear group objects in the context of tangent categories. We define the general linear group of a differential object as a classifier of the Kleisli parametrized linear automorphisms of the differential object, a class of maps that generalize linear automorphisms in a suitable sense. We prove that such a group can be entirely constructed via a tangent triple equalizer and use this characterization to prove that the Lie algebra of general linear group objects is isomorphic to the Lie algebra of linear endomorphisms.
\end{abstract}
