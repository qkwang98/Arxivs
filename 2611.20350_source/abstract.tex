Our goal in this paper is to work towards developing a framework for characterizing dualities of weak categorical structures. It is often the case that duality involutions are characterized by relations which are more general than $f^2=1$. Moreover, it is also true that many dualities cannot be expressed using involutions alone. We develop a framework of relative involutions and relative inverses to express these dualities at least within categorical and bicategorical structures. In particular, we utilize this framework to express the dualities of virtual double categories and some important constructions on them. In the second part, we explore the problem of slicing and consider how dualities of monoidal structures interact with slice constructions. We end by providing a construction for both enriched slice model categories and enriched coslice model categories.
