\begin{abstract}
We count nilpotent matrices in the incidence algebra of a finite poset over
a finite field that belong to a prescribed conjugacy class for the adjoint
action of the general linear group. We prove that these
counts depend only on the comparability graph of the poset. We introduce
shuffle--prefix posets, a class containing all finite interval orders, and
give explicit formulas for their nilpotent Jordan counts.
These counts are polynomials with integer coefficients in the field
size, valid over every finite field. Finally, Fourier duality relates
conjugacy-class counts in a coordinate subspace to those in the subspace
supported on the complementary matrix positions, whenever one subspace
consists entirely of nilpotent matrices.
\end{abstract}
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} incidence algebras, finite fields, posets, Hall--Littlewood functions, Fourier duality.
