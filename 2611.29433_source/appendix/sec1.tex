\subsection{Conditions for the existence of a left adjoint}

The content of this subsection is standard general category theory and contains
nothing specific to this paper.
We refer to \cite{MacLane} for proofs and state only the assertions, in the form
used in the body.

\begin{definition}
  Let $F$ be a functor from a category $C$ to a category $D$.
  Let $Y$ be an object of $D$, let $G_Y$ be an object of $C$, and let
  \[
    \eta_Y:Y\to F(G_Y)
  \]
  be a morphism of $D$.
  We say that $\eta_Y$ is a \textbf{universal morphism from $Y$ to $F$} if the
  following holds.

  For every object $X$ of $C$ and every morphism $u:Y\to F(X)$ of $D$, there is
  a unique morphism
  \[
    u^\sharp:G_Y\to X
  \]
  of $C$ satisfying
  \[
    u=F(u^\sharp)\circ\eta_Y .
  \]

  We call the above condition the \textbf{universal property} of
  $\eta_Y:Y\to F(G_Y)$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:left-adjunction-existence-from-universality}
  Let $F$ be a functor from a category $C$ to a category $D$.
  If for every object $Y$ of $D$ one can choose a universal morphism
  \[
    \eta_Y:Y\to F(G_Y)
  \]
  from $Y$ to $F$, then a left adjoint
  \[
    G:D\to C
  \]
  of $F$ exists.
  Moreover the action of $G$ on morphisms is uniquely determined by the
  universal property of each $\eta_Y$.
\end{theorem}

\noindent
For a proof see \cite{MacLane}, Chapter IV, \S1, Theorem 2.
The argument runs as follows: assigning $G_Y$ to an object $Y$, and to a
morphism $f:Y\to Y'$ the morphism determined by the universal property of
$\eta_{Y'}\circ f$, makes $G$ a functor, and
$\Psi_{Y,X}(u):=F(u)\circ\eta_Y$ gives a natural bijection
$\operatorname{Hom}_C(G(Y),X)\cong\operatorname{Hom}_D(Y,F(X))$.
