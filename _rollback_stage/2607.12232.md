# Contractions and applications of crystal skeletons: Young quasisymmetric and Stanley symmetric functions

keywords: [crystal skeleton, quasicrystal skeleton, Young quasisymmetric Schur functions, Stanley symmetric functions, dual equivalence graph]
related_paper: "Assaf's dual equivalence graphs"
one_liner_summary: "Decomposes the crystal skeleton into quasicrystal skeleton components whose characters are Young quasisymmetric Schur functions, characterizes the edges between components, and shows that further contracting the quasicrystal skeleton yields the Bruhat order on standard Young tableaux, with applications to Stanley symmetric functions."

## 1.  🎯 核心定位

- **解决的问题**：理解 crystal skeleton（Assaf 引入的 dual equivalence graph 的晶体推广）的内部结构——它如何分解为更小的 quasicrystal skeleton 组件，以及这些组件如何通过收缩产生 Bruhat 序。
- **大图景位置**：在对称函数理论与晶体基之间建立桥梁。crystal skeleton 的 character 是 Schur 函数，其进一步分解为 quasicrystal skeleton 后 character 变为 Young quasisymmetric Schur 函数，再收缩得 Bruhat 序——形成一条清晰的层级链。
- **主要贡献**：
  1. 定义 quasicrystal skeleton 为 crystal skeleton 的子图，其 character 为 Young quasisymmetric Schur 函数
  2. 刻画 quasicrystal skeleton 中改变组件的边（component change edges）
  3. 将 quasicrystal skeleton 收缩得到标准 Young 表上的 Bruhat 序，并应用于 Stanley 对称函数
- **关键先驱工作**：Assaf 的 dual equivalence graphs (DEG)；Morse–Schilling 的晶体 Stanley 对称函数；Luoto–Mykytiuk–van Willigenburg 的 Young quasisymmetric Schur 函数
- **结果边界**：quasicrystal skeleton 的连通性依赖于底层 quasicrystal 的结构；Bruhat 序的收缩仅在部分情形精确成立
- **开放问题**：quasicrystal skeleton 中 component change edges 的计数公式；连通性充要条件

## 2.  📐 主要定理

> **Theorem (Component Change Edges, Theorem in §4.2)**: In the quasicrystal skeleton, the crystal edges $f_i$ that change quasicrystal components are precisely those for which there is a pairing between letters in the $i$-th and $(i+1)$-st factors of the corresponding increasing factorization.

> **Theorem (Bruhat Order from Quasicrystal Skeleton, §4.4)**: Contracting each quasicrystal skeleton component to a single vertex yields a poset isomorphic to a quotient of the Bruhat order on standard Young tableaux of shape $\lambda$.

### 2.1.  证明思路

- 第一步：利用 $\varphi$ 双射将标准 Young composition tableaux (SYCT) 与标准 Young 表 (SYT) 对应
- 第二步：通过 Edelman–Greene 双射将 increasing factorizations 翻译为 $(P,Q)$ 表对
- 第三步：晶体算子 $f_i$ 在 recording tableau $Q$ 上的作用刻画 quasicrystal 间边，而配对条件精确决定何时 $f_i$ 改变组件
- 第四步：收缩 quasicrystal skeleton 时，Bruhat 序由 descent 比较来定义

## 3.  🔑 关键组件

### 3.1.  关键引理

> **Lemma (Superstandard filling)**: The superstandard filling of a composition $\alpha$ is always a Young composition tableau. — *[直接验证 triple condition]*

> **Corollary (Reduced words index quasicrystals)**: Increasing factorizations $a,b \in \mathcal{IW}_w^\ell$ are in the same quasicrystal if and only if $\mathsf{red}(a)=\mathsf{red}(b)$, i.e., they share the same underlying reduced word of $w^{-1}$.

### 3.2.  关键定义

> **Definition (Quasicrystal Skeleton)**: For a fixed highest weight crystal $B(\lambda)$, the quasicrystal skeleton is the subgraph of the crystal skeleton where edges $f_i$ are retained only when they do NOT create a pairing between letters $i$ and $i+1$ in the corresponding increasing factorization.
> **动机**：DEG 中 quasicrystal 是"不可再分"的组件；crystal skeleton 是 DEG 的自然晶体推广，quasicrystal skeleton 是其中"DEG-like"的精细分解。

> **Definition (Semistandard Young Composition Tableau, SYCT)**: A filling of the composition diagram of $\alpha$ (French notation) such that: (1) rows weakly increase left to right; (2) leftmost column strictly increases bottom to top; (3) triple condition: if $r'>r$ with $(r,c+1)$ in diagram and $T(r,c+1) \geq T(r',c)$, then $T(r,c+1) > T(r',c+1)$.
> **动机**：这些 tableaux 的 generating function 是 Young quasisymmetric Schur 函数，其 descent 统计给出 fundamental quasisymmetric 展开。

> **Definition ($\varphi$ bijection)**: $\varphi: \bigcup_\alpha \mathsf{SYCT}(\alpha) \to \mathsf{SYT}(\lambda)$ maps a Young composition tableau to a standard Young tableau by arranging all columns in increasing order bottom to top. The inverse inserts entries column by column.
> **动机**：这是理解 crystal skeleton（顶点为 SYT(λ)）与 quasicrystal skeleton（顶点对应 SYCT）之间关系的核心工具。

## 4.  💡 理解辅助

- **贯穿例子**：$\lambda=(3,2,1)$ 的 crystal skeleton 和 quasicrystal skeleton 图示贯穿全文。对应的 SYT 和 SYCT 分别展示在 Figure 1 和 Section 5 中。
