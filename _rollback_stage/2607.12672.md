# Negative K-Theory and Hodge Theory

keywords: [negative K-theory, cdh cohomology, mixed Hodge modules, singular varieties, Bloch's conjecture]
related_paper: "Bloch (1986) — Algebraic cycles and higher K-theory"
one_liner_summary: "Establishes a deep connection between negative algebraic K-theory of singular varieties and Hodge-theoretic invariants via weight-graded cdh cohomology of mixed Hodge modules."

## 1.  🎯 核心定位

- **解决的问题**：对于奇异代数簇 X，负 K 群 K_{−n}(X) (n ≥ 0) 的计算极其困难——本文通过 cdh 上同调与混合 Hodge 模的桥梁，将 K_{−n}(X) 的 Q-向量空间维度与 Hodge 理论不变量联系起来。
- **大图景位置**：推广 Bloch 猜想（光滑簇的 CHⁿ 与 Hodge 上同调关系）到奇异簇，填补负 K 理论与 Hodge 理论之间的鸿沟。
- **主要贡献**：
  - 建立负 K 群与 cdh 超上同调 H^i_cdh(X, Q(j)) 的谱序列关系
  - 对 klt 型孤立奇点，证明 H^{n+2}_cdh(X, Q(2)) = 0（权重 2），H^{n+1}_cdh(X, Q(1)) 的维数公式
  - 对一般奇异簇，给出 K_{−n}(X)_Q = 0 的充分条件（n > dim X + d，d 为奇点复杂性参数）
- **关键先驱工作**：Bloch (Adv. Math. 1986), Haesemeyer (K-theory and cdh cohomology), Saito (mixed Hodge modules), Cortiñas–Haesemeyer–Walker–Weibel (K-theory of singular varieties)
- **结果边界**：主要结果在 Q-系数下成立；积分系数下存在反例（Kummer 三体）；klt 奇点假设对权重消失定理至关重要
- **开放问题**：积分系数下的类比；权重 ≥ 3 的 cdh 上同调的几何解释；与 Voevodsky 母题上同调的关系
- **应用与推广**：计算具体奇异簇（锥面、有理双点等）的负 K 群维数；连接 birational geometry 与 algebraic K-theory

## 2.  📐 主要定理

> **Theorem A** (Weight 2 vanishing): Let X be a normal projective variety of dimension n with at most klt isolated singularities. Then H^{n+2}_cdh(X, Q(2)) = 0.

> **Theorem B** (Weight 1 computation): Under the same hypotheses as Theorem A, dim_Q H^{n+1}_cdh(X, Q(1)) equals the number of exceptional divisors over the singular locus (i.e., the rank of the local class group at the singular points).

> **Theorem C** (Negative K-groups): For X with klt isolated singularities, dim_Q K_{−m}(X)_Q is determined for m = 0, 1, 2 by the weight-graded pieces of cdh cohomology of mixed Hodge modules on a resolution.

> **Theorem D** (General vanishing): For any variety X of dimension n, K_{−m}(X)_Q = 0 for m > n + d, where d is the maximal dimension of the singular locus of a cdh covering.

### 2.1.  证明思路

- **第一步**：利用 Cortiñas–Haesemeyer–Walker–Weibel 的纤维化定理，将 K 理论的 cdh 下降谱序列转换为 Z(i)-系数的 cdh 超上同调
- **第二步**：对光滑簇情形，通过 Bloch 公式 Hⁱ_cdh(X, Q(j)) ≅ Gr^W_{−j} H^{i+j}_{Hodge}(X)，将 cdh 上同调翻译为混合 Hodge 结构
- **第三步**：奇异簇情形通过奇点解消 π: Ỹ → X，利用混合 Hodge 模的导出范畴构造三角 D 并计算 cdh 上同调——关键引理 α, β, γ 分别控制 Chow 群限制映射、相交配对、以及曲线到点的满射性
- **第四步**：针对 klt 孤立奇点进行维数计算，利用 Hodge 结构的权重滤波和 Deligne 的混合 Hodge 理论消去高阶项

## 3.  🔑 关键组件

### 3.1.  关键引理

> **Lemma γ** (Curve-to-point surjectivity): The restriction map CH₁(Ỹ, Q) → CH₁(X, Q) is surjective for a resolution Ỹ → X with klt singularities. — *本质使用极小模型纲领*

> **Lemma β** (Intersection pairing): The pairing CH²(Ỹ) × CH^(n−2)(Ỹ) → CH^n(Ỹ) ≅ Q induced by intersection descends to a nondegenerate pairing on the image of CH²(X) → CH²(Ỹ). — *建立 Chow 群内积结构*

> **Lemma α** (Chow group restriction): For klt singularities, the image of CH²(X) → CH²(Ỹ) equals the kernel of the exceptional divisor pairing. — *刻画 Chow 群限制映射的像*

### 3.2.  关键定义

> **cdh topology and cdh cohomology**: The completely decomposed h-topology, a Grothendieck topology on the category of schemes generated by Nisnevich covers and abstract blow-ups.
> **动机**：cdh 拓扑是代数 K 理论下降性质的天然框架，Cortiñas 等证明了 K 理论满足 cdh 下降

> **Mixed Hodge modules on singular varieties**: Saito's theory extending variations of mixed Hodge structures to the singular setting.
> **动机**：奇异簇上定义 Hodge 理论的唯一系统框架，权重滤波提供 cdh 上同调的分次结构

## 4.  💡 理解辅助

- **贯穿例子**：三维 klt 孤立奇点（如锥面 over a del Pezzo surface）：
  - Theorem A ⇒ H⁵_cdh(X, Q(2)) = 0
  - Theorem B ⇒ H⁴_cdh(X, Q(1)) 维数 = 例外除子个数 = ρ(Ỹ/X)
  - Theorem C ⇒ dim K_{−1}(X)_Q = dim H⁴_cdh(X, Q(1)) + dim H³_cdh(X, Q(1))
- **Kummer 三体反例**：X = 三个点缩为一点的曲线，积分系数下 K_{−1}(X) ≠ 0 而 Hodge 理论给出零——说明 Q-系数假设不可省略
