## 📐 主要定理

> **Theorem (Stable case)**: Every smooth stable Higgs family on a compact Kähler manifold satisfying the non-Abelian Hodge numerical conditions admits a global smooth harmonic metric. A global Hermitian-Einstein determinant metric always exists, and prescribing it makes the harmonic metric unique.
>
> **Theorem (Deformation)**: For a Higgs deformation η, the normalized first metric variation satisfies L_h s = -S_h(η) with s = -G_h S_h(η), where L_h is a self-adjoint elliptic Jacobi operator whose trace-free kernel vanishes by stability.
