## 📐 主要定理

> **Theorem (intro_thm:Chern character descends)**: The Chern character ch: Sh(Λ) → Diff_Q[[Λ]] descends to an isomorphism of λ-rings if and only if the Todd class td is invertible and there exists f = td·g for some g.

> **Theorem (intro_thm:Operators)**: K_f and A_g are Frobenius rings. Moreover, every finitely generated Frobenius ring can be realized as K_f or A_g for appropriate shift/differential operators.

> **Theorem (thm:map-descends-iff-hrr)**: The Chern character map descends to the Frobenius quotient K_f if and only if the Todd class satisfies a Hirzebruch--Riemann--Roch-type condition relating f to td.

> **Theorem (thm:grr)**: The Grothendieck--Riemann--Roch theorem holds for the standard graded setting: the Chern character intertwines the λ-ring structures and the multiplicative maps defined on K_f and A_g.

> **Theorem (thm:lambda-ring)**: K_f and A_g carry natural λ-ring structures, and the Chern character is a λ-ring homomorphism when the HRR condition is satisfied.

### 证明思路

- 第一步：从 Frobenius 代数出发，利用移位算子（shift operator）和微分算子（differential operator）对 Frobenius 代数取商，构造 K_f 和 A_g。证明这些商环继承原 Frobenius 代数的 Frobenius 配对。
- 第二步：在 K_f 和 A_g 上建立 K 理论和 Chow 理论的类似物。定义 Sh(Λ)（K 理论来源）和 Diff_Q[[Λ]]（Chow/同调目标），构造 Chern 特征映射 ch。

> **Frobenius 商 K_f / A_g**：给定 Frobenius 代数 (R,Φ)，K_f 由移位算子 f 作用下的商 R / (f·R) 定义，A_g 由微分算子 g 作用下的类似构造定义。
