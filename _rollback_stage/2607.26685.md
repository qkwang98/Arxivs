## 📐 主要定理

> **Main Theorem A (hguhtuoiwntoir3)**: Let K and L be arithmetically equivalent number fields (i.e., ζ_K(s) = ζ_L(s)). Then for all n ≥ 1:
> 1. K_{2n-1}(O_K) ≅ K_{2n-1}(O_L) (odd K-groups are isomorphic).
> 2. There exists a natural comparison map inducing an isomorphism up to finite kernel and cokernel for even K-groups (almost K-equivalence).

> **Main Theorem B**: If K and L are locally integrally equivalent (having the same local zeta factors at all places) and totally imaginary, then K_n(O_K) ≅ K_n(O_L) for all n ≥ 2.

> **算术等价（arithmetic equivalence）**：两个数域 K 和 L 称为算术等价，若 ζ_K(s)=ζ_L(s)。等价于范数形式相同（Perlis 定理）。
