## 📐 主要定理

> **Theorem (thm:op_sys_as_modules)**: The category of nonunital operator systems is equivalent to the category of left CMat-modules. Similarly, the category of dual nonunital operator systems is equivalent to the category of right CMat-modules.

> **Separation Theorem**: In any Bénabou cosmos with a regular bigenerator and coseparator, representable modules form a coseparating family.

> **Representation Theorem / Extension Theorem**: Abstract versions of the classical theorems for operator systems — that every nonunital operator system embeds into a unital one, and that morphisms on subsystems extend — are proven in the enriched module framework.

### 证明思路

- 第一步：定义 enriched cosmos CMat（紧致矩阵代数范畴 enriched over 自身），使得 CMat 上的模对应为将 CMat-对象映射到向量空间并满足特定左/右作用的函子。
- 第二步：利用 enriched Yoneda 引理和 co-Yoneda 引理，证明每个 CMat-模可表为可表模的余极限，进而与具体的算子系统（作为具体矩阵代数）一一对应。
- 第三步：通过引入 ROBan cosmos（regular bigenerator + Bénabou cosmos），利用 enriched dual Yoneda、¬ 否定函子和配对态射，建立分离定理、表示定理和扩张定理的范畴化版本。
