## 📐 主要定理

> **Theorem (main)**: A graph parameter f is a mixed partition function if and only if f has exponentially bounded edge-connection rank. That is, there exists a constant R > 0 such that the edge-connection rank of f grows at most as 2^{R·k}, where k is the number of edges.
