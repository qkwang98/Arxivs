# A topological proof that compact Hausdorff spaces are not finitely co-concrete

### 2.1.  定义

> **Definition 1**: A category $\mathcal{K}$ is *finitely concrete* if it admits a faithful functor $\mathcal{K} \to \mathbf{Set}$ that preserves sequential colimits, i.e., a faithful $\aleph_0$-accessible functor.

### 2.2.  主定理

> **Theorem**: $\mathbf{CompHaus}^{\mathrm{op}}$ is not finitely concrete. Moreover, there exists a specific sequential colimit in $\mathbf{CompHaus}^{\mathrm{op}}$ that no faithful functor $\mathbf{CompHaus}^{\mathrm{op}} \to \mathbf{Set}$ preserves.
