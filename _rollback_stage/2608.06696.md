## 2.  📐 主要定理

> **Theorem 3.10** (Relative Interval Tilting): For a staircase poset, there exists a tilting complex whose indecomposable summands are naturally indexed by "relative intervals" — contiguous subposets that correspond to corners of the higher Auslander staircase.

> **Theorem 5.1** (Gorenstein Projective-Injectives = Nakayama): For a rational staircase poset with coprime parameters (r,s), the category of Gorenstein projective-injective modules is a Nakayama algebra whose Auslander-Reiten quiver is a single cyclic path.

> **Theorem 5.6** (Tilt Equivalence → CLR): The Nakayama algebra N(r,s) is tilting equivalent to the incidence algebra of the rational staircase poset. In particular, A_{n+1} and A_n are derived equivalent, proving the CLR conjecture in the coprime case.
