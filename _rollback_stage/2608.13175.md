# $g$-vector fans and picture categories for 0-Auslander extriangulated categories

keywords: [0-Auslander extriangulated category, polyhedral fan, g-vector fan, partitioned fan, picture category]
related_paper: "Silting theory and g-vector fans for triangulated categories"
one_liner_summary: "本文将 g-vector fan 理论推广到具投射 silting 对象的 0-Auslander extriangulated 范畴，证明 g-向量构成有理单纯多面体 fan，并以 partitioned fan 统一 picture category 的构造。"
## 🎯 核心定位

- **解决的问题**：在 0-Auslander extriangulated 范畴 C（带投射 silting 对象 T）中，如何建立 g-vector fan（极大锥与 silting 对象对应）并给出 picture category 的统一范畴化描述。
- **大图景位置**：这是 silting 理论 / g-vector fan 纲领的推广：以往 g-vector fan 建立在 tilting/silting 对象与 τ-cluster 范畴上，本文将其延伸到 0-Auslander extriangulated 范畴，并以 partitioned fan 的语言统一 picture category（此前 dg 与 τ-cluster 两种构造）。
- **主要贡献**：①证明存在有理且单纯的 g-vector fan $\partfan{\calC}$（Theorem IsFan）；②定义 picture category W(C)，证明其统一 τ-cluster morphism category 与 dg picture category（Theorem introthm1）；③建立 partitioned fan 态射与 picture category 函子的对应（Theorem introthm2），并给出 Galois 连接与 silting reduction 的配套结果。
- **关键先驱工作**：依赖 silting 理论（Aihara-Iyama）、τ-tilting 理论（Adachi-Iyama-Reiten）、Auslander 代数与 0-Auslander 范畴、cluster 范畴的 picture category（Kai23），以及 extriangulated 范畴的公理化（Herschend-Liu-Nakaoka）。
- **结果边界**：构造基于 Setting 中的 0-Auslander extriangulated 范畴与 projective silting 对象；对无 projective-injective 对象需特殊处理；picture category 的 cubical 结构依赖 [Kai23] 的定理，dg 增强情形的等价有额外前提。
- **开放问题**：作者未单独列出 open problems，但从 prop:STTWgeneralisation 与 partitioned fan 语言可推断：对更高 Auslander 阶数（d-Auslander）与一般 extriangulated 范畴的推广是自然方向。
- **应用与推广**：可用于 τ-cluster 范畴的分类、cluster 代数系数的 fan 描述、以及通过 W(C) 与 cubical category 建立离散 Morse 理论式的不变量；partitioned fan 态射语言有望统一多类组合范畴。

## 📐 主要定理

> **Theorem (IsFan)**: Let $\calC$ be as in \Cref{setting}. Then $\partfan{\calC}$ is a rational and simplicial polyhedral fan.

> **Theorem (introthm1)**: Let $\calC$ {and $T$} be as in \Cref{setting} and let $\Lambda$ be any finite-dimensional $k$-algebra. There is a well-defined picture category $\Wfrak(\calC)$ satisfying: (1) The $\tau$-cluster morphism category of $\Lambda$ is equivalent to $\Wfrak(\Ktwo(\proj \Lambda))$; (2) If $\calC$ does not admit nonzero projective-injective objects, then $\Wfrak(\Ktwo(\proj \End_{\calC}(T))) \simeq \Wfrak(\calC)$; (3) If $\calC$ admits an exact dg enhancement in the sense of [cite], then the picture category of $\calC$ as defined in [cite] is equivalent to $\Wfrak(\calC)$.

> **Theorem (introthm2)**: Let $\calC_1$ and $\calC_2$ be two categories satisfying \Cref{setting}. Assume that there is a morphism of partitioned fans $f: (\calC_1, (\Pfrak_{\thick})_1) \to (\calC_2, (\Pfrak_{\thick})_2)$, then: (1) There is a functor $\mathfrak{C} f: \Wfrak(\calC_1) \to \Wfrak(\calC_2)$; (2) If $f$ is injective on cones, then $\mathfrak{C} f$ is faithful; (3) If $f$ is surjective on cones, then $\mathfrak{C} f$ is dense. Conversely, let $G: \calC_1 \to \calC_2$ be an extriangulated functor. Assume that $G$ preserves presilting objects and maps the projective silting object in $\calC_1$ to the projective silting object in $\calC_2$, then: (4) $G$ induces a morphism of partitioned fans $g$; (5) If $G$ is injective on presilting objects, then $g$ is injective on cones; (6) If $G$ is surjective on presilting objects, then $g$ is surjective on cones.

### 证明思路

- 第一步：对 presilting 对象 U 构造其 g-向量，证明 presilting 对象的 g-向量线性无关（lem:IsFan），从而可定义以这些向量张成的锥族。
- 第二步：利用 silting reduction（prop:siltred）与 Horseshoe Lemma 验证锥族满足 fan 公理（相交为公共面、有限性），并证明极大锥一一对应 silting 对象。
- 第三步：以 partitioned fan 为骨架定义 picture category W(C)，验证其与 τ-cluster morphism category 及 dg picture category 的等价，并证明 partitioned fan 态射诱导出函子（thm:partmorphgivesfunctor）及其忠实/稠密性质。

## 🔑 关键组件

### 关键引理

> **Lemma (lem:IsFan)**: The g-vectors of presilting objects are linearly independent and form the rays of a fan; the maximal cones of $\partfan{\calC}$ correspond bijectively to silting objects in $\calC$. — *[新证]*

> **Proposition (prop:samePartition)**: The picture category $\Wfrak(\calC)$ is equivalent to the $\tau$-cluster morphism category of $\Lambda$, and (under an exact dg enhancement) to the dg picture category. — *[新证，统一以往构造]*

### 关键定义

> **Definition (g-vector fan)**: For a presilting object $U$ in $\calC$, its g-vector is the class in the Grothendieck-type lattice $N_{\calC}$; the g-vector fan $\partfan{\calC}$ is the polyhedral fan whose maximal cones are the cones spanned by the g-vectors of silting objects.
> **动机**：把 silting 对象的组合信息编码为格点凸几何中的 fan，使对偶理论、突变与分类问题转化为多面体几何问题，是连接表示论与组合学的桥梁。

> **Definition (partitioned fan 与 picture category)**: A partitioned fan is a polyhedral fan together with a partition $\Pfrak_{\thick}$ of its cones satisfying admissibility conditions; the picture category $\Wfrak(\calC)$ has as objects the equivalence classes of basic presilting objects and as morphisms certain partitioned-fan morphisms, generalizing the cluster morphism category.
> **动机**：picture category 将 τ-cluster morphism category 中「picture 元素 = 三角」的组合直觉抽象为 partitioned fan 语言，从而同时涵盖 dg 与 τ-cluster 两种构造，实现统一。
