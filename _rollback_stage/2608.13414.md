# Synthetic Buildings for Finite Groups

keywords: [synthetic building, simplicial complex, finite group, minimal synthetic building, p-subgroups complex]
related_paper: "P. J. Webb, A split exact sequence for cohomology (1987)"
one_liner_summary: "本文用 p-局部不动点条件给出有限群 synthetic building 的纯拓扑定义，将其作为 Tits building 与 p-子群复形的统一框架，证明唯一性、极小性并建立 join、乘积、限制、悬浮等构造与 reduced Lefschetz 模的轨道公式。"

## 1.  🎯 核心定位

- **解决的问题**：如何不借助 BN-对与 Lie 型结构，仅用有限群作用在单纯复形上的不动点拓扑条件来刻画"building"，并使 Tits building、p-子群复形与 strongly p-embedded 子群等对象统一在同一个定义之下。
- **大图景位置**：这是 Tits building 理论的同伦论推广，也是 Brown、Thévenaz 与 Webb 关于 p-子群复形与 minimal building 工作的延伸；它把表示论（reduced Lefschetz 模、Green 环）与有限群拓扑联系起来，试图为任意有限群提供"拓扑秩"的类比。
- **主要贡献**：1) 引入 synthetic building 与 weak synthetic building、极小性与 slender 性、exotic building 等新概念；2) 证明结构定理（增广链复形同伦等价于有限长度投射复形、Lefschetz 模是虚拟投射模）与轨道层面的 Tate 上同调公式；3) 证明 p-群定理、strongly p-embedded 刻画定理、S_5 与 Lie 秩 2 唯一性定理，并构造无限族不同伦型的一维 slender synthetic buildings；4) 建立 join 半群与乘积幺半群运算、限制与 SB 函子，给出 reduced Lefschetz 模多重度的轨道求和公式。
- **关键先驱工作**：Tits building 理论；Quillen 的 p-子群复形与 F-理论；Webb 的 p-subgroups complex 及其分裂正合序列（Web1987）；Brown 与 Thévenaz 的 minimal building 与 Bouc 的 biset 范畴方法；Benson 的 Green 环与模表示论教科书。
- **结果边界**：当 p 不整除 |G| 时定义平凡（每个单形稳定子阶不被 p 整除，第二条条件无法满足）；极小 synthetic building 的同伦型是否有限仍是猜想；强 p-嵌入子群只在极特殊群里存在，无限族构造依赖该传输条件；乘积一般不保持 synthetic building 性质（仅对角作用时成立），文中以 S_3×S_3 的例子说明。
- **开放问题**：作者明确提出五个猜想：1) 每个有限群与素数 p 的极小 synthetic building 只有有限多个等变同伦型；2) p-子群复形同伦等价于某个极小 synthetic building，且其维数是所有极小 synthetic building 维数的上界（由此给出 Lie 秩的推广）；3) 所有极小 synthetic building 都是 p-子群复形的满射像；4) 特征 p 的 Lie 型群的唯一非平凡极小 synthetic building 是 Tits building；5) 关于 slender 子复形 Δ_Y 与 O_p(G)≠1 的判别猜想。
- **应用与推广**：Lefschetz 模轨道公式可直接用于计算有限群模表示中的投射覆盖多重度；synthetic building 可作为有限群等变同伦理论的不变量；join 与乘积运算在等变同伦类上给出半群/幺半群结构，提示可定义类似 K-理论的代数对象；1 维 slender synthetic building 的无限族构造为 fusion 系统与 Borel 型子群研究提供了新的复形来源。

## 2.  📐 主要定理

> **Definition (synthetic building)**: A G-simplicial complex Δ is a synthetic building for G at the prime p if the following conditions hold: (1) The fixed point space Δ^P is N_G(P)-equivariantly contractible for all nonidentity p-subgroups P ≤ G, and (2) Every simplex stabilizer has order divisible by p. Δ is a weak synthetic building if condition (1) is satisfied (and not necessarily condition (2)).

> **Theorem (structure of chain complex)**: Let Δ be a weak synthetic building for a finite group G at a prime p and let R be the p-adic integers Z_p or a field of characteristic p. Let C̃_•(Δ) be the augmented chain complex of Δ over R. Then (1) C̃_•(Δ) is chain homotopy equivalent to a finite length complex of finitely generated projective RG-modules; explicitly, C̃_•(Δ) ≅ D_• ⊕ P_• where D_• is a contractible complex and P_• is a finite length complex of finitely generated projective RG-modules. (2) The reduced Lefschetz module L̃(Δ) = Σ_{i ≥ -1} C̃_i(Δ) is a virtual projective module in the Green ring of finitely generated RG-modules.

> **Theorem (p-group theorem)**: Let G be a finite group. (1) If G is a p-group then every synthetic building for G at the prime p is equivariantly contractible. (2) O_p(G) ≠ 1 if and only if every minimal synthetic building at the prime p is equivariantly contractible.

> **Theorem (strongly p-embedded)**: (1) If a group G has a disconnected synthetic building then the setwise stabilizer of a component is a strongly p-embedded subgroup, and that component is a synthetic building for the stabilizer. (2) If a group G has a strongly p-embedded subgroup K and Δ is a synthetic building for K, then Δ↑_K^G, defined to be the disjoint union of copies of Δ indexed by the cosets G/K, with G-action given by the action on induced G-sets (K/H)↑_K^G ≅ G/H, is a synthetic building for G.

> **Theorem (S_5 uniqueness)**: At p = 2 the symmetric group S_5 has a unique non-trivial minimal synthetic building.

> **Theorem (infinite family)**: Let G be a finite group that contains a strongly p-embedded subgroup. Then there is an infinite family of 1-dimensional slender synthetic buildings for G at the prime p, all of distinct homotopy types.

### 2.1.  证明思路

- 第一步：p-group 情形直接利用任意非平凡 p-子群不动点复形等变可缩的强条件，将 Δ 逐步压缩到单点；O_p(G) ≠ 1 的等价性则通过 p-子群复形的等变可缩性及 minimal building 的极小性刻画。
- 第二步：strongly p-embedded 刻画依赖对连通分支稳定子的分析，证明固定点的 p-局部信息与轨道空间 G\Δ 的结构完全由稳定子族决定；诱导构造则逐单形检查稳定子阶被 p 整除与不动点可缩性两条公理。
- 第三步：无限族构造利用传输条件 N_G(⟨g⟩,T) ⊆ TS 保证轨道图（orbit diagram）的可实现性，配合唯一性定理证明这些一维 slender buildings 两两不同伦。

## 3.  🔑 关键组件

### 3.1.  关键引理

> **Lemma (orbit formula for Lefschetz modules)**: Let k be a field of characteristic p and Δ a weak synthetic building. For each simple kG-module S, the multiplicity of the projective cover P_S as a summand of L̃(Δ) equals (Σ_{σ ∈ [Δ̃/G]} (−1)^{dim σ} (multiplicity of P_k for G_σ in S↓_{G_σ})) / dim End_{kG}(S), where Δ̃ is Δ together with an artificial extra simplex stabilized by G in dimension −1. — *[新证：Webb 的 Propn 5.3 特例此前无证明，本文补全]*

### 3.2.  关键定义

> **Definition (slender / orbit diagram)**: A G-simplicial complex Δ is slender if the orbit space G\Δ is contractible. An orbit diagram for Δ is a picture of G\Δ with the orbits of simplices labeled by representative orbit stabilizers.
> **动机**：slender 性把"复形整体形状"压缩到轨道空间的可缩性，使一维 synthetic buildings 的分类转化为带稳定子标号的轨道树分类，从而可枚举。

> **Definition (SB functor and restriction)**: Given a weak synthetic building Δ for G at the prime p, SB(Δ) is the subset of the simplices of Δ consisting of the simplices whose stabilizers have order divisible by p. For H ≤ G, the restriction Res_H^G(Δ) = SB(Δ↓_H^G) is again a synthetic building.
> **动机**：去掉稳定子阶不被 p 整除的"多余"单形，恰好修复合成本身不保持 synthetic building 性质的问题，使限制与对角作用下的 join/乘积都能回到 synthetic building 范畴。

## 4.  💡 理解辅助

- **贯穿例子**：对称群 S_5 在 p=2 时的唯一非平凡极小 synthetic building，以及 S_{2p} 的 0 维与 1 维 minimal buildings；用 A_7、M_11 的几何与 S_3×S_3（p=2）的 join/乘积例子展示 exotic buildings 与轨道图；C_3^d ⋊ C_2 一族给出 d+1 个同伦不同的极小 synthetic buildings，是枚举轨道图的最小规模样本。
