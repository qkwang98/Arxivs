## 📐 主要定理

> **Theorem (Descent obstruction)**: Let $(\mathfrak{A},U,c,H)$ be a Universe~A structure with an extensionally coherent height, and let $(A_{\mathrm{src}},A_{\mathrm{tgt}},f)$ be a comparison problem over an object $X$. Let $H^{\mathrm{ext}}$ be the extensional height induced on $\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{ext}}^{\kappa}$. Then the images of $A_{\mathrm{src}}$ and $A_{\mathrm{tgt}}$ under the extensional collapse are transported with cost controlled by $\mu_{\mathrm{tot}}$.

> **Theorem (The support term cannot be dropped)**: There are no constants $C>0$ and $B\ge0$ such that for every prime $p\ge5$ there exists a height-admissible descent datum $\delta_p\colon(L_p,\mathrm{NF}_{L_p}((\mathcal{E}_p)_{L_p}))\longrightarrow(\mathbb{Q},\mathrm{NF}_{\mathbb{Q}}(\mathcal{E}_p))$ satisfying $\mu_{\mathrm{tot}}(\delta_p)\le C\log2+B$. Consequently a descent bound of the form $C_{\mathrm{desc}}\log\ell+B_{\mathrm{desc},K}$, carrying no tame support term, fails.

> **Theorem (The discriminant height is a transport log-volume)**: For every number field $K$, the assignment $\mathrm{Vol}^{A}_{\mathrm{Szp}}(\mathcal{E})\coloneqq h_\Delta(\mathcal{E})$ is a transport Szpiro log-volume. One may take $A=1$, $B=0$, and $C_0=1$.

> **Conjecture (Intensional BSD, rank form)**: Let $\mathcal{E}$ be a Mordell--Weil package over $K$ with lattice of rank $r$. Then there exists a positive real constant $C_\mathcal{E}$ such that $\Pi_\mathcal{E}(X)\sim C_\mathcal{E}(\log X)^r$ as $X\to\infty$. Equivalently, the rank $r$ is the growth exponent of the partial Euler transport.
