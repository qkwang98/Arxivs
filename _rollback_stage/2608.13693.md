## 📐 主要定理

> **Theorem (Exact completion)**: There is an equivalence of categories $\mathrm{Ex}(\mathbf{P})\simeq\mathbf{PC}$ mapping each Polish group $G$ to the group with a Polish cover $G/N$ where $N$ is the trivial subgroup of $G$.

> **Theorem (Thick subcategories)**: Let $\mathcal{S}$ be a thick subcategory of the category $\mathbf{P}$ of Polish groups. Then the equivalence $\mathrm{Ex}(\mathbf{P})\simeq\mathbf{PC}$ restricts to an equivalence between $\mathrm{Ex}(\mathcal{S})$ and the full subcategory of $\mathbf{PC}$ spanned by $\mathcal{S}$-groups with a Polish cover.

> **Theorem (Brown)**: Suppose that $1\longrightarrow M\xrightarrow{\iota}P\xrightarrow{\pi}G\longrightarrow 1$ is an exact sequence of standard Borel groups, where $M$ and $G$ are Polish. Suppose, in addition, that $\pi$ admits a Borel right inverse. Then $P$ admits a unique Polish group topology inducing its given Borel structure. With respect to this topology, $\iota$ is a topological embedding and $\pi$ is a continuous open surjection.
