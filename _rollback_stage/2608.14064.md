## 📐 主要定理

> **Theorem 3.9**: Let $\mathcal{H}$ be a $B$-Hopf algebroid, and $P$ a right $\mathcal{H}$-comodule algebra. The following are equivalent: (i) $P$ is anti-cleft; (ii) $P$ is an anti-right $\mathcal{H}$-Galois extension of $N=P^{\mathrm{co}\mathcal{H}}$ that admits a normal basis; (iii) $P\cong N\#_\sigma\mathcal{H}$ is a crossed product with an invertible cocycle.

> **Theorem (Ehresmann = generalized Connes--Moscovici)**: Let $(\lambda,\sigma)$ be an invertible cocycle of $\mathcal{H}$ over $N$. The Connes-Moscovici Hopf algebroid $\mathcal{L}$ is an $N$-Hopf algebroid. It is the left Ehresmann Hopf algebroid $L(N\#_\sigma\mathcal{H},\mathcal{H})$ of the right anti-Galois extension $N\#_\sigma\mathcal{H}$: a left coaction of $\mathcal{L}$ on $N\#_\sigma\mathcal{H}$ is given explicitly in the text.

> **Theorem 4.8**: If $(\mathcal{L},\mathcal{R},S)$ is a Hopf algebroid with bijective antipode, then the definitions of cleft extension given here and in Böhm--Brzękowski are equivalent.

- 第一步：定义 cleft 扩张为存在 $B$-线性余模截面 $\gamma$（以及反版本 anti-cleft），利用截面与卷积逆构造协变作用与正规基同构。
