## 2.  📐 主要定理

> **Theorem 2.3**: Let $\mathfrak{g}$ be a symmetrizable Kac--Moody Lie algebra. Let $\lambda,\mu,\nu\in P^+$ and $w\in W$. Let $\pi_w:V(\mu)\twoheadrightarrow V_w(\mu)$ be the orthogonal projection onto the Demazure module $V_w(\mu)$ with respect to the Shapovalov form. Then $\dim(\pi_w(\mathbb{M}^{\nu}_{\lambda\mu}))=c_{\lambda\mu}^{\nu}(w)$.

> **Theorem (Presentation)**: Let $\mathfrak{g}$ be a finite dimensional semisimple Lie algebra or a symmetric Kac--Moody Lie algebra. Consider $K(\lambda,w,\mu)=U\mathfrak{g}(v_\lambda\otimes v_{w\mu})$. Let $J$ be the left ideal of $U\mathfrak{g}$ generated by: (i) $e_\alpha^{p_\alpha(w\mu)+1}$, $\alpha\in\Delta_{\mathrm{pos}}^{\mathrm{re}}$; (ii) $f_\alpha^{\langle\lambda,\alpha^\vee\rangle+1}$, $\alpha\in\Delta_{\mathrm{pos}}^{\mathrm{re}}$; (iii) $x_\beta$, $\beta\in\Delta_{\mathrm{pos}}^{\mathrm{im}}$; (iv) $h-\langle w\mu,h\rangle 1$, $h\in\mathfrak{h}$; (v) $\mathfrak{g}_\beta$, $\beta\in\Delta_{\mathrm{pos}}^{\mathrm{im}}$. Then $K(\lambda,w,\mu)\cong U\mathfrak{g}/J$.

> **Theorem (Hom isomorphism)**: Let $\mathfrak{g}$ be a finite-dimensional semisimple Lie algebra or a symmetric Kac--Moody algebra. Then $\mathrm{res}:\mathrm{Hom}_{U\mathfrak{g}}(K(\lambda,w,\mu),V(\nu))\to\mathrm{Hom}_{U\mathfrak{b}}(v_\lambda\otimes V_w(\mu),V(\nu))$ is an isomorphism of vector spaces.
