## 📐 主要定理

> **Theorem (st.BGA)**: Let $A$ be a non-local Brauer graph algebra, and let $B$ be a basic symmetric algebra with no semisimple summands. If $B$ and $A$ are stably equivalent, then $B$ is also a Brauer graph algebra.

> **Theorem (st.M-BGA)**: Let $A$ be a Brauer graph algebra. Then for any basic algebra $B$ with no semisimple summands, if $B$ and $A$ are stably equivalent of Morita type, then $B$ is also a Brauer graph algebra.

> **Corollary (Derived equivalence)**: Let $A$ be a Brauer graph algebra. Then for any basic algebra $B$, if $B$ and $A$ are derived equivalent, then $B$ is also a Brauer graph algebra.

### 证明思路

- 第一步：研究 Brauer 图代数 $A$ 中由 deformed loop 诱导的 tube 上的模结构：tube 口部模 $X$ 是最大链模，$\Omega_A(X)$ 是例外 band 模，并证明对任意串模 $Y$ 有 $\dim_k\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}_A(X,Y)=\dim_k\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}_A(X,\Omega_A(Y))$ 等关键不变量。
- 第二步：证明非例外 band 模 $M$ 满足 $\dim_k\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}_A(M,Y)>n$ 对任意 $n$（存在不落在 rank 1 tube 的串模），而 tube 口部模的 Hom 维数受控，从而在稳定等价下区分 tube 模与非例外 band 模。
- 第三步：设 $B$ 与 $A$ 稳定等价（Morita 型），由 Asashiba--Zarzuela 定理 $B$ 是 stably biserial；结合对称性得到 symmetric stably biserial，其 Brauer 图表述中若有 deformed loops 则会产生 tube 模与 band 模维数不变量矛盾，从而排除之，$B$ 即为 Brauer 图代数。

## 🔑 关键组件

### 关键引理

> **Lemma (equal-dimension)**: Let $\Lambda$ be a symmetric stably biserial algebra, and let $X$ be a string module lying at the mouth of a homogeneous tube corresponding to a deformed loop in the Brauer graph of $\Lambda$. Then $X$ is a maximal chain module and $\Omega_\Lambda(X)$ is an exceptional band module. For any string $\Lambda$-module $Y$, we have $\dim_k\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}_\Lambda(X,Y)=\dim_k\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}_\Lambda(X,\Omega_\Lambda(Y))$.

> **动机**：Brauer 图代数是一大类几何起源的对称代数，其稳定等价封闭性说明该类的表示论刚性。
