- **大图景位置**：开轨道存在性是可约表示理论中的经典问题（Kac 的轨道闭包、stable modules 等）；本文将之与箭图表示论结合：$\Aut_Q(M)$ 作用于 $\Ext^1_Q(M,M)^*$，轨道闭包对应 Lusztig 幂零簇的分支，刚性即分支的刚性。

## 2.  📐 主要定理

> **Theorem (greedy criterion)**: Let $G$ be a connected solvable algebraic group acting rationally on a finite-dimensional vector space $U$, and fix a $G$-stable complete flag. The successive-quotient procedure either: (1) terminates successfully and produces a vector with open $G$-orbit, or (2) terminates at a quotient $U_k$ on which $G$ has no open orbit. In the successful case, the number of active coordinates is minimal among all open-orbit vectors and the image of a generic stabilizer in the maximal torus quotient is determined by the active set.

> **Theorem (rigidity reduction)**: Let $Q$ be a Dynkin quiver and $M$ a multiplicity-free module with root set $\Sigma$, $\#\Sigma=n$, and let $Z_M$ be the corresponding irreducible component. Fix an adapted enumeration of a block basis of $E$ and run the greedy procedure for the action of $\Aut_Q(M)$ on $E$. Then rigidity of $Z_M$ is reduced to a rank test together with an acyclicity condition on the graph of active extension coordinates; the connected components of the resulting forest determine the generic indecomposable decomposition.

> **Theorem (rigid iff smooth)**: The following conditions are equivalent: (1) $\IM_\sigma$ is rigid; (2) $\IM_\sigma$ is strongly rigid; (3) $\sigma$ is smooth.

### 2.1.  证明思路

- 第一步：把开轨道问题化为逐次商：沿 $G$-稳定旗取逐次商 $U_k$，在每一步用"活动坐标集 $\Phi$"编码支撑；贪心规则：若行 $x_1,\dots,x_i$ 的矩阵 $M(\lambda)$ 对 $\supp\lambda=\Phi_{i-1}$ 线性相关则激活 $x_i$，否则跳过；若 $\Phi_i$ 含环则中止。
- 第二步：证明判停的两种出口（成功产出开轨道向量 / 商上无开轨道）互斥且穷尽；用一般稳定子 $S_\Psi$ 与极大环面商 $\pSat(\Psi)$ 的像描述开轨道的一般参数，证明活动集大小与选择无关（不依赖旗与特征基）。
- 第三步：在 quiver 应用中将 $E=\Ext^1_Q(M,M)^*$ 的元素与 $M$ 的 $\Pi$-模结构对应（$E$ 的块分解、稳定子 $\Aut_\Pi(\tilde M)$），刚性 $\Leftrightarrow$ $\rk M(\lambda)=\#\U$（对支撑为 covers 的 $\lambda$）+ 覆盖图无环，从而把刚性归约为线性代数检验与图论检验。

## 3.  🔑 关键组件

### 3.1.  关键命题

> **Proposition (infinitesimal criterion)**: The greedy infinitesimal procedure succeeds if and only if there exists an open separable orbit in $U$. In this case, the algorithm stepwise coincides with the non-infinitesimal version. If the greedy infinitesimal procedure aborts at $k$, then there is no open separable orbit in $U_k$.

> **Proposition (module structure correspondence)**: (1) The elements of $E$ correspond to the $\Pi$-module structures on $M$ that are compatible as a $Q$-module. (2) If $y\in E$ corresponds to $\tilde M$, then the stabilizer in $\Aut_Q(M)$ of $y$ is $\Aut_\Pi(\tilde M)$. (3) As an $\Aut_Q(M)$-module, $E$ is isomorphic to $\Ext^1_Q(M,M)^*$.

> **Corollary (enumeration independence)**: If the procedure succeeds, then for every $w\in\orbit$, the images of $G_w$ and $S_\Psi$ in $G^{\diag}$ coincide; and $\#\Phi=\rk G-\rk G_w=N-\dim(\rad{G}\cdot w)$. In particular the active set size is independent of the choices of flag and eigenbasis.

### 3.2.  关键定义

> **Definition (Successive-quotient / greedy procedure)**: 沿 $G$-稳定完全旗 $0=U_0\subset U_1\subset\cdots$ 取商 $U_k=U/U_k$，维护活动坐标集 $\Phi$：在步骤 $i$，若 $x_1,\dots,x_i$ 的行在支撑 $\Phi_{i-1}$ 上线性相关则激活 $x_i$，否则保持；若 $\Phi_i$ 在"ones"上含环则中止。成功时 $\supp v=\Phi$ 的 $v$ 具有稠密轨道。
> **动机**：贪心把"存在开轨道"这一整体性质化为沿旗的逐次线性相关性检验，是可计算判定。

> **Definition (Active extension coordinates / cover graph)**: 在 quiver 应用中，$E=\Ext^1_Q(M,M)^*$ 的基元素按 containment 序（$x\sqsubset y$）组织；参数 $\lambda_y$ 出现在 $x$ 的行中当且仅当 $y\sqsubset x$；活动坐标的覆盖图（covers 作为 edges）无环时刚性成立。
> **动机**：参数的包含序编码扩张坐标间的依赖，活动集与覆盖森林把表示论的刚性翻译为纯组合对象。
