## 2.  📐 主要定理

> **Theorem (main)**: The Lie algebra $\mathfrak{g}=\sum_{i=-3}^3\mathfrak{g}_i$ constructed as the Tanaka prolongation of $\mathfrak{g}_-+\mathfrak{g}_0$ above is the split real form of the exceptional simple Lie algebra of type $\mathrm{G}_2$.

> **Theorem (1:3 ratio)**: The rolling distribution $D_{r_0/r_1}$ admits a Lie algebra of local infinitesimal symmetries isomorphic to $\mathfrak{g}$ if and only if $\tfrac{r_0}{r_1}\in\{3,\tfrac{1}{3}\}$.

> **Corollary (kinematic 1:3)**: In the setting of Lemma setrolleye, either $r_0=1$ and $r_1=\tfrac13$, or $r_0=\tfrac13$ and $r_1=1$; equivalently the ratio condition is precisely the intersection condition of the two one-parameter subgroups visible from the rolling sphere perspective.
